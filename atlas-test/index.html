About the EPR Atlas

The EPR Atlas is a public-records map created by Enid Public Record to help residents explore public property, facilities, infrastructure, and major public investments associated with Enid, Oklahoma.

Enid Public Record is independent and is not an official City of Enid website.

Legal & evidence notice

Informational use. The Atlas is compiled from public records and other documented sources for reporting and public-information purposes. It is not a survey, title opinion, legal description, engineering plan, or substitute for an official record.

Location and boundaries. Parcel geometry, infrastructure alignments, facility points, wellfield locators, and other mapped features may be generalized or approximate where the evidence does not support survey-grade precision. Qualifiers shown in the Atlas are part of the record.

Changes and verification. Ownership, condition, status, and public records can change. For a legal, financial, construction, or property decision, verify the underlying official record with the responsible public agency.

Privacy. The Atlas requests device location only when you tap the location control. The Atlas does not include code that saves your precise device location to Enid Public Record.
