PRIVACY NOTE

Email is useful, but it is not anonymous.

Ordinary email can reveal information such as your email address and message metadata to the email providers involved. Do not send highly sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

Enid Public Record does not promise confidentiality simply because a message is labeled “private” or “anonymous.” If a future secure tip channel is added, it will be clearly identified on this page.
