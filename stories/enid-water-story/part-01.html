AUGUST 14

Before There Was Enid, There Was Water

Government Springs gives the story a beginning before the Land Run. The opening chapter then moves into the new town's first years and the remarkably early public discussion about building a municipal waterworks.

  • Verified: Government Springs predates Enid as a watering/camping location.
  • Verified: authentic early Enid photographs exist from the Land Run era.
  • Verified: by June 1894, Enid newspaper discussion described waterworks as a needed improvement.
  • Do not overstate: direct proof that Government Springs was the principal drinking source for Enid residents in 1893 has not been located.
