Before There Was Enid, There Was Water

Government Springs gives the story a beginning before the Land Run. The opening chapter then moves into the new town's first years and the remarkably early public discussion about building a municipal waterworks.

  • Before Enid existed, Government Springs was already known as a watering and camping place along the Chisholm Trail era route.
  • Photographs from the Land Run era show how quickly Enid appeared on the prairie after September 16, 1893.
  • By June 1894, less than nine months after the Land Run, an Enid newspaper was already describing municipal waterworks as a needed improvement.

The surviving record does not yet establish that Government Springs was the principal drinking-water source for Enid residents in 1893. The series treats that question as unresolved rather than assuming an answer.
