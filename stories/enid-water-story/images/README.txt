Rights rule
-----------
GREEN  = public domain, user-owned/original, or written permission received -> may be stored and displayed.
YELLOW = rights unclear / permission pending -> link to archive record; do not store/display image here yet.
RED    = restricted or permission denied -> do not use.

For every image added here, update ../image-sources.txt with:
- filename
- story part
- title/description
- creator/photographer
- source institution
- source URL
- date
- rights status
- permission date/reference, if applicable
- required credit line
- alt text
- crop/edit notes

Accessibility
-------------
Every meaningful image used on a page must have concise descriptive alt text or an adjacent text equivalent.
Charts/maps must not rely on color alone to convey meaning.
