AUGUST 18

The Canton Story

Canton is one of the most misunderstood parts of Enid's water history. The federal record describes storage reserved for Enid, decades of Oklahoma City use under federal contracts, an Enid–OKC agreement in 1990, and congressional reassignment through WRDA 1990.

Important wording: this story concerns federally authorized municipal water-supply storage in a federal reservoir. That is not the same thing as saying Enid “owned half of Canton Lake.”
  • Verified: Oklahoma City began federal five-year storage-use contracts in 1955 because Enid was not accessing its storage.
  • Verified: Enid and Oklahoma City reached an agreement dated April 17, 1990.
  • Verified: WRDA 1990 made federal reassignment subject to an agreement between the cities.
  • Do not publish: the circulating $750,000 payment claim remains unverified.
Publication note: This series is built from a verified fact ledger. Claims that remain unresolved are labeled instead of guessed.
