AUGUST 18

The Canton Story

Canton is one of the most misunderstood parts of Enid's water history. The federal record describes storage reserved for Enid, decades of Oklahoma City use under federal contracts, an Enid–OKC agreement in 1990, and congressional reassignment through WRDA 1990.

Important wording: this story concerns federally authorized municipal water-supply storage in a federal reservoir. That is not the same thing as saying Enid “owned half of Canton Lake.”
  • Oklahoma City began using Canton storage under federal five-year contracts in 1955 because Enid was not accessing its municipal storage allocation.
  • Enid and Oklahoma City reached an agreement dated April 17, 1990.
  • The Water Resources Development Act of 1990 made federal reassignment of the storage subject to an agreement between the cities.

One important question remains unresolved in the records reviewed so far: the exact financial consideration Enid received under the 1990 agreement. A circulating $750,000 figure has not been verified and is not used here as fact.
