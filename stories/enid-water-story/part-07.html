AUGUST 20

Why Kaw?

Engineers compared additional groundwater, new local reservoirs and Kaw Lake. Kaw was not the cheapest option studied. Its strongest strategic advantage was diversification: Enid could retain groundwater while adding a large surface-water source.

Selected 2014 planning-level comparisons. These are study-era comparisons, not final project costs.
AlternativePlanning takeawayKey tradeoff
More groundwaterLowest estimated construction costMany more wells/rights; continued groundwater dependence
New local reservoirLarge potential yieldHigher cost, water-quality treatment, permitting/filling uncertainty; Vance bird-strike concern for Hennessey concept
KawSurface-water diversification and scalabilityHigher cost than groundwater-only and major long-distance infrastructure
