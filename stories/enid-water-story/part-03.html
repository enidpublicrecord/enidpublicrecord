What's Under the Water Works?

Modern Water Works map/photograph

Public display pending final imagery-rights verification. The article remains complete without relying on the image.

Underground galleries

Federal records document a central pump pit and underground gallery wells at Enid's main plant, expanded in 1918.

A giant brick well

USGS independently documented a later municipal well 20 feet in diameter and 47 feet deep.

The “frog pond” mystery

A 1921 newspaper described an old open reserve reservoir. A West Chestnut municipal reservoir was still reported in 1951. The modern pond may be the same feature or a reworked descendant, but that identity is not yet proven.

Publication note: This series is built from a verified fact ledger. Claims that remain unresolved are labeled instead of guessed.
