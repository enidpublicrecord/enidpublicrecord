AUGUST 19

The Wells Weren't Empty. So Why More Water?

The engineering record does not support the simplistic claim that Enid's aquifers were about to become empty. The concern was long-term reliability: aging wells, localized drawdown, drought limitations, water quality, interference between wells and future demand.

Estimated sustainable existing-well supply~7.3 MGD
2014 reduced-demand projection for 205010.9 MGD
2014 high-demand projection for 205018.4 MGD

Groundwater expansion remained a viable alternative—and a cheaper one in the 2014 study—but it would require many additional wells, rights, pipelines and treatment infrastructure.
