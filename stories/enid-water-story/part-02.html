Enid Builds a Waterworks

In 1900, Enid voters approved a $40,000 municipal waterworks program. The project quickly became a story of growth, fire protection, expansion, engineering disputes and outside attention.

  • In 1900, Enid voters approved a $40,000 municipal waterworks bond proposition.
  • A national engineering trade publication reported that the new system would draw from a well described as 26 feet in diameter.
  • By 1904, firefighting capacity was already a concern as the growing city demanded more from the system.
  • In 1911, a New York trade journal sought technical information about Enid’s waterworks plans for publication.

The 26-foot measurement is reported in a contemporary engineering publication; the precise location of that well has not yet been independently established.
