Enid Builds a Waterworks

In 1900, Enid voters approved a $40,000 municipal waterworks program. The project quickly became a story of growth, fire protection, expansion, engineering disputes and outside attention.

  • Verified: $40,000 waterworks bond proposition approved in 1900.
  • Verified report: a national engineering trade publication described a 26-foot-diameter source well.
  • Context: by 1904, the existing system's firefighting capacity was already a concern.
  • 1911: a New York trade journal sought technical information about Enid's waterworks plans.
Publication note: This series is built from a verified fact ledger. Claims that remain unresolved are labeled instead of guessed.
