08

AUGUST 21 • RIBBON CUTTING

133 Years Later

Initial treatment capacity10.5 MGD
Designed full build-out21 MGD
Raw-water line~70 miles

The initial Kaw phase is designed to supplement Enid's groundwater, not replace it. The City's stated goal is to reduce average-day demand on regional aquifers by roughly 35% while preserving the well system as part of the supply portfolio.

Cost wording: current reporting describes the initial 10.5-MGD phase at approximately $330 million and the full 21-MGD build-out at approximately $475 million. Those are different scopes. Final audited project closeout is not yet available.
Publication note: This series is built from a verified fact ledger. Claims that remain unresolved are labeled instead of guessed.
