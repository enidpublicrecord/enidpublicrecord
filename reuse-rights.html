Reuse & Rights

We want the public record to travel. We also want original EPR work and third-party archival material to be used responsibly.

Linking is encouraged

You are welcome to share links to Enid Public Record articles, story pages and source libraries on social media, websites, email and other platforms.

Original EPR material

© 2026 Enid Public Record. All rights reserved in original EPR reporting, writing, graphics, site design and EPR-produced audio. Republishing, redistributing, syndicating or re-uploading substantial EPR content requires permission.

For permission requests, contact contact@enidpublicrecord.com.

Quoting and legal rights

Nothing on this page is intended to limit quotation, fair use, public-domain use or other rights provided by applicable law. When quoting EPR, clear attribution and a link to the original page are appreciated.

Archival photographs and third-party records

Historical photographs, documents and other third-party materials are credited to their sources and rights holders. Permission granted to Enid Public Record to display a third-party item does not transfer that permission to another person or publication. Follow the original-record link and contact the relevant archive or rights holder when reuse permission is needed.

Audio

EPR-produced narration is provided for listening on the article page. Please share the article link rather than downloading and redistributing the audio file. Permission is required for substantial republication, re-uploading, syndication or incorporation of EPR-produced audio into another work, except as otherwise allowed by law.

Corrections and updates

Material corrections and significant updates are documented when they affect the meaning or reliability of a published story. To report a possible error, email contact@enidpublicrecord.com.
