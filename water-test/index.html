The eight words

Permit class matters

Regular and term permits should not be collapsed into one simplistic cutoff queue.

Rights are not storage

An Oklahoma state water right and a U.S. Army Corps storage contract are different legal layers.

Use is separate again

Authorized AFY is not the same as documented annual withdrawal.

What this app is showing

Freshness

The date shown in the status strip is the publication date of this public dataset, not a claim that every source is live or continuously updated.

Qualifiers
