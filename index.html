OUR MISSION

Make the public record usable.

Most people do not have hours to search years of agendas, minutes, budgets, contracts and technical reports. We turn that scattered information into a readable path from “What is this?” to “Now I understand.”

01

Find the record

Collect primary documents, meeting records, budgets, studies, reporting and other reliable sources.

02

Translate lengthy or technical material without stripping away important detail.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Follow the record wherever it leads.

Enid Public Record is not built to tell readers what conclusion to reach. Relevant evidence that supports a project belongs in the record. Relevant evidence that raises concerns belongs there too.

We aim to distinguish verified fact, analysis, opinion and unanswered questions, cite meaningful claims and correct mistakes transparently.
