EDITORIAL STANDARD

Follow the record wherever it leads.

Enid Public Record is not built to tell readers what conclusion to reach. Relevant evidence that supports a project belongs in the record. Relevant evidence that raises concerns belongs there too.

We aim to distinguish verified fact, analysis, opinion and unanswered questions, cite meaningful claims and correct mistakes transparently.
